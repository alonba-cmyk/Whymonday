# Approved examples: event session titles (Elevate)

Real, Alon-approved outputs. Use them as the standard for `hook : descriptor` session titles, one per audience, same offering with the hook bent to each department's appetite.

## The Elevate set

- **IT** (wants to lead responsibly): "IT leading the AI charge: Driving responsible AI adoption across the entire organization with the AI work platform."
- **PMO & Ops** (wants delivery): "Every project, delivered: What execution looks like when agents handle operations on the AI work platform."
- **Enterprise** (wants scale): "From individual wins to company-wide impact: How enterprises turn local AI wins into business results on the AI work platform."
- **Marketing** (wants volume): "Demand without limits: How marketing teams execute more campaigns with monday agents."

## Enterprise title: why this one (approved)

**Locked:** *From individual wins to company-wide impact: How enterprises turn local AI wins into business results on the AI work platform.*

It is the hybrid of the two options Alon liked:
- Keeps the flattering "individual wins" framing (the current state is a success, just localized, not a deficiency). Serves the "flatter, never blame" rule.
- Descriptor advances to "business results" instead of restating scale, so the hook and descriptor each say something new (no repetition).
- "company-wide impact" avoids echoing "enterprise" against "How enterprises."

### Altitude lesson (from the naming round)
For senior/Enterprise sessions, keep the "before" state in a `From X to Y` hook **strategic**, never **tactical**:
- Too tactical: "From scattered pilots to company-wide impact" ("pilots" is PoC/rollout language; drops the altitude of a keynote-level session).
- Right altitude: "From individual wins..." or "From personal AI..." (both mirror the vision doc's "AI stays personal" tension and stay strategic).
