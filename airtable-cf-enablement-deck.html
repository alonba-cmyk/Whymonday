01 Context

Airtable just changed hands.

On August 4, Bending Spoons acquired Airtable for $1.285 billion. Overnight, 500,000+ organizations, including 80% of the Fortune 100, have their critical work on a platform under new ownership.

  • The acquirer's pattern is known. Bending Spoons (Evernote, WeTransfer, Meetup, AOL, Eventbrite) buys software and monetizes hard. Airtable customers are reading the same headlines.
  • Uncertainty creates openness. Roadmap, pricing, and support are now question marks. Customers will take a call they wouldn't have a month ago.
  • The window is now. Every competitor sees the same opening. Speed and specificity win the accounts.
"Airtable is a database you build. monday.com is the AI Workspace the whole team can use."Our positioning is already live on the monday.com vs. Airtable page. This is the line to carry into every conversation.

05 Why we win

Airtable is a database you build.
monday is a platform your team uses.

Five reasons, straight from the monday.com vs. Airtable page. The through-line: it's not what the tool can do, it's who on the team can actually use it.

1

Anyone can use it, not just the builder

Visual boards vs. Airtable's relational database model. Airtable stays with the technical person who built it; monday drives adoption across the whole org.

2

Ready out of the box, not built from scratch

Timeline, Gantt, Kanban, Calendar and 200+ native integrations included. Airtable needs setup, paid extensions for Gantt/Timeline, and Zapier or Make to connect.

3

AI and agents built in, not bolted on

Every team member uses monday's AI from day one, with agents that execute tasks 24/7. Airtable's AI is capable but still needs setup, structure, and someone who knows the system.

4

Built for the long run, enterprise-grade

250,000+ customers, 60% of the Fortune 500, 24/7 support on every plan. SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001, GDPR covered. Airtable offers email and community support only.

5

More out of the box, no upgrade pressure

Airtable's seat-based pricing scales fast and has risen sharply. monday gives more at every tier, and migration takes minutes with dedicated support.
