Signup, reimagined

An agent shows up in the first minute.

The homepage already promises people and agents, working together to deliver results. The signup should prove it before you finish signing up, not seven screens later.

The problem

The agent arrives too late.

Today the flow asks what brings you here, then your role, then your focus, then to invite your team, all before an agent ever appears. By then the promise has gone cold.

The principle

One question. Then an agent, working.

Ask the one thing that matters, what you want to get done, and use it to put a relevant agent on screen immediately. Everything else can wait until after the value lands.

    The argument, at a glance

    Seven gates become three.

    Same signup goals, resequenced around the promise. The three separate questions collapse into one intent, the agent moves to the front, and inviting the team waits until after the value lands.

    Todaytime-to-agent: slow
    7 gates before value
    • 01 Sign up
    • 02 What brings you here?
    • 03 What's your role?
    • 04 What to focus on first?
    • 05 Invite your team
    • 06 Pick your first agent
    • 07 Agent sets things up
    • 08 You reach the board
    Reimaginedtime-to-agent: step 2
    3 gates, agent up front
    • 01 Sign up
    • 02 What do you want to get done? (one question)
    • 03 Meet the agent matched to it
    • 04 Land in the board, agent already working
    • + Invite your team (after value, optional)
    Why it works

    Three decisions doing the heavy lifting.

    01
    Intent replaces the questionnaire

    One question, "what do you want to get done," carries the signal that role plus focus plus "what brings you here" used to spread across three screens.

    02
    The agent is the reward, not a setup step

    Instead of "pick an agent" as a chore, the right agent is offered to you, by name, already knowing the job you came to do.

    03
    Team invite earns its place

    You invite people once you have something worth inviting them to. It moves out of the critical path and stops blocking the first win.
